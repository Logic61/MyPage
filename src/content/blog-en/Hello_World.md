TL;DR: This is an Astro blog, mostly for technical writing. My essays, stories, and poems live on my old blog at <https://logic61.github.io/>.

I'd already made a blog in Hexo with the ParticleX theme. It looked rather nice, I thought. But the more I posted, the more it turned into a diary. Fair enough for a blog, I suppose, though I wanted a personal site I could someday use as a calling card. So the first thing you'd see ought to be a little about me, and the whole thing ought to look clean and uncluttered, and so on. I went ahead and built this site in Astro, and it can do more than the old one. But I suppose the old blog is like the wife who saw you through the lean years: I couldn't bring myself to leave it behind. So I gave the two sites a room each, as it were. Technical pieces here, and essays, stories, poems, and the like back on the old blog.
