* * *

**"So interesting."**

I turned my cheek to look, his gaze fixed on the man and woman sitting side-by-side on the diagonal across the subway car.

They were separated by only an aisle.

* * *

**"Are you giving up?"** I asked; he used to like that woman.

**"What do you mean giving up? I never even thought about it."**

**"This—"**

After a moment of silence, I seemed to have touched a chord of his heart.

* * *

**"I think there might be something wrong with her psychology because of her experiences."**

**"Oh?"**

**"This—ah, something I've only read about in books actually happened to her."**

So I began to make various guesses.

**"Actually, never mind. Let me tell you from the beginning."**

* * *

She was very young then, but what she went through would be very heavy even for us now.

When she was in fourth grade, her father suddenly brought home a woman. She asked curiously:

> ."Dad, who is this?"

Her father heard this, suddenly seemed to remember something, showed a look of annoyance, and then quickly changed into a smile, saying:

> ."This is Dad's friend. You can call her Auntie."

The woman also forced a smile:

> ."Come, Auntie has something for you to eat."

So she received a piece of candy.

> ."Don't tell Mom that Auntie came," said the father.

But she was very puzzled:

> ."Why?"

> ."Children, why do you ask so many things? Do you still want to eat candy?"

> ."Yes."

> ."Then don't tell Mom."

**"Okay."**

She unwrapped the candy and ate it happily, then quietly slipped into the room, pressed her ear against the half-open door, and could hear bursts of laughter coming from the living room.

* * *

**"Okay, it's about time, I should go."** the woman said, then gave her father a flying kiss, and the father returned a flying kiss. She found it very interesting; were adults always saying goodbye like this?

The next day, when she said goodbye to her friends after school, she also imitated them and gave her classmate a flying kiss.

> ."What are you doing?"

> ."I saw my dad saying goodbye to others like this. It's an adult custom!"

The parent standing next to her classmate looked at her with strange eyes.

That night, while doing her homework, she heard her mother's angry voice from the living room:

> ."How come? Flying kisses, really romantic!"

> ."What flying kisses."

> ."Your daughter was giving flying kisses to someone at school, and her parent saw it. Do you know what she said? I imitate Dad! Dad! You think I don't know you're involved with that fox spirit? If it weren't for our daughter, I would have divorced you long ago!"

> ."Ah, wife, don't say that. She's just a friend to me. It doesn't conflict with our marital relationship."

> ."Heh, friends!"

> ."Okay, okay. We are indeed mutually fond, but I still love you! Why can't you accept her?"

> ."I! Listen, is what you're saying human language!"

> ."How is it not human language? Even though I married you, I still have the right to pursue happiness, right? Accepting her won't be a big deal. If you could be friends with her, and the three of us could live together happily, that would be wonderful."

> ."Go dream your dreams!"

“…”

* * *

Hearing all this, although she didn't understand everything, she generally understood. Suddenly, she felt incredibly scared. She opened the door and rushed out, standing between her parents who were arguing:

> ."Stop arguing!"

> ."What are you, a little child, interfering with? Get away!" Her father roared, clenching his fists, his eyes slightly red.

> ."Why are you so harsh with the child!" Her mother's hair was messy, and she cried out.

> ."What's wrong with me being a bit rough? You don't earn much money day after day. If you leave, see how you live."

> ."You!" Her mother burst into tears.

> ."Even if I hit you, what's wrong? And for the sake of our daughter, I think you're doing it for money. If you want money, then just open your heart a little, just accept a woman, is it that hard?" After saying this, he raised his palm.

> ."Lowly! You!"

."Who is lowly?!"

Her mother was slapped hard, and her left cheek was bright red.

> ."Who do you say is lowly?!" Another slap, and blood flowed uncontrollably from the corner of the mother's lips, and her body recoiled repeatedly.

> ."Who is lowly in the end?!! Ah!" Another slap.

> ."Waa—" She couldn't bear it and burst into tears.

> ."What are you crying for? It's so noisy." He continued to hit her.

> ."Dad—please! Please! Don't hit me anymore."

> ."You want to be hit too?"

His father turned his palm towards her.

* * *

A similar scene happened every few days. By the end, for some unknown reason, even the mother who used to dote on her started hitting her. Even during relatively peaceful times, the house seemed to be in a vacuum, where no sound could be heard, and the atmosphere was just heavy. Although she was young, she knew that this situation was hard to maintain.

* * *

Finally, several months later, her parents divorced. Her mother took money from her father and gave up the custody rights that should have belonged to her. So she began to live with her father.

* * *

**"This—is truly pitiful."** I sighed.

**"Alas, indeed. And strangely, she has almost no resentment towards her father; instead, she is very close to him. The last time I went to her place to play ball, I saw her fooling around with her father—yes, fooling around, being extremely intimate, which struck me as very strange. The relationship between them was unexpectedly good, the kind of intimacy that was almost like a couple, and I couldn't see at all that she had been..."**

**"I cannot understand."**

**“Later, you also know that she got into our school. She had just started, and she was involved with the boys from the P.E. class, and eventually, there was only one, they were really dating. But after a year, there was no contact.”**

**“In the second year, I happened to be involved in a rumor with someone in the class. At this time, one afternoon, during some activity, both of us injured our feet, and then we chatted there, chatted for a long time, and then for some unknown reason, our relationship got better. Later, he seemed to enter my life, everywhere, and then it was spread by you all like this.”**

**“So, that means it was her who initiated it.”**

**“Yes. But later, when we played a soccer match, we lost in the penalty shootout. As the goalkeeper, I was hit hard. Really, I felt more upset than anyone. I almost put my life into this match. I knelt on the field for a long time. The sun was bright, and I was covered in sweat, and then I walked out slowly. But I couldn't recover, I just kept kneeling in front of the goal, and she was always accompanying me from behind. The sun cast her shadow on my shoulder. Although she stood silently, she really entered my heart. I even felt that if I didn't leave, she might be able to accompany me to the end of the world. Haha, just kidding. Later, I felt it had been a while, and it was time to go to class. I wanted to stand up, but my legs couldn't stand after kneeling for so long. She helped me walk up a hundred steps. All the classmates who tried to talk to me on the way were all turned down by her with a finger to her lips, and she was late with me, and we were questioned by the teacher. If I had had the strength then, I would have hugged her tightly.”**

**“Indeed, anyone would be moved.”**

**“Mmm. Then another half year passed. Maybe it was my hot and cold behavior, but everything was unpredicted. Her communication with me became less and less, but she chatted with the boys around her desk in her own classroom about everything, asking this or borrowing that. This is abnormal. At the beginning of the story, I was still a poor guy involved in a rumor with someone. But given my relationship with someone at that time, you could say we were a couple. At this time, I and someone were playing the roles of her parents. She used the excuse of injuring my feet to frantically bring our relationship closer. What do you think of this? Yes, at this time, he was playing the role of his stepmother, the one who took over. Later, I played the role of her birth mother alone, and she deliberately chatted animatedly with many opposite-sex people, playing the role of his father, making me, who liked her, suffer. She went to the person sitting next to her, but that person was different from every boy she approached before. It seemed she really liked him, really liked him, I could see it. The look in your eyes when you like someone cannot be hidden. When you like someone, every move hides details. When the teacher called his name, she raised her head faster than the person himself. When I was in the vortex of events, I was also dizzy. But at least in this matter, only I, the third party outside the Mount Lu, could see things more clearly, but these are all my deductions. He, haha, seems like the me of the past.”**

**“Speaking of which, it seems quite interesting. The class, which seems to be filled with competition, is secretly surging with waves other than studying, roaring in waves.”**

**“Don't get so sentimental like writing an essay. But indeed, some things cannot be seen unless you dig deep. The teacher probably can only see the surface of the class; it's already quite good to be able to figure out the academic situation of each student.”**

**“But her personality is indeed very strange. After you told me, I feel it's very hard to figure out. If this were used for reading comprehension, it would be terrible.”**

**“That's true. Like fog, but I probably don't know much either.”**

**“Isn't this a lot?”**

**“Not much.”**

**“Okay. It really is like fog.”**

* * *

At night, I saw a bird flying from the trees into the sky. I don't know if there were clouds or fog; it was unclear at night, but I thought, the trees must be green.
