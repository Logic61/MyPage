Coming back to life.

By afternoon I thought I might as well go out for another ride, maybe still riding the momentum of yesterday's outing. It was already past three. Perhaps I'd give the Garden Expo Park a miss and just wander.

So I rode along the sea. First I came to a park with a lake at its center and a tall, splendid hotel rising beside it.

![A hotel beside the lake](../../blog/%E6%B8%B8%E8%AE%B0/images/%E5%9B%AD%E5%8D%9A%E8%8B%917.8/651a7f495df17760f7656fa67b716670_720.jpg)

Someone with quite an eye chose the spot for that hotel. It looks very good from here. There must be a fine view from up there, too. Across the lake is the road. Across the road is the sea.

![The road and the sea beyond the lake](../../blog/%E6%B8%B8%E8%AE%B0/images/%E5%9B%AD%E5%8D%9A%E8%8B%917.8/ead7352a8aacf0852fc34ab1bcf9c778.jpg)

All that room between earth and sky really does open something up inside you. I was riding around the lake when there was a sudden rustling in the bushes, and a few egrets flew out.

![Along the lake at Egret Island](../../blog/%E6%B8%B8%E8%AE%B0/images/%E5%9B%AD%E5%8D%9A%E8%8B%917.8/18c159cdcb3583a019a9932a7085fe59.jpeg)

Egret Island lives up to its name, then. You seem to see egrets everywhere.

I rode on and came to another glittering hotel.

![A grand hotel seen from the footbridge](../../blog/%E6%B8%B8%E8%AE%B0/images/%E5%9B%AD%E5%8D%9A%E8%8B%917.8/d830c6dfed02702ce378c270b42bc8ac.jpg)

The view really is good. They call it a hotel, but it looks as grand as any castle. I took this from the footbridge. Once across it, I was on the road to the sea.

![The route from the footbridge toward the sea](../../blog/%E6%B8%B8%E8%AE%B0/images/%E5%9B%AD%E5%8D%9A%E8%8B%917.8/810af426080aa0a908a54f13c0d74c58.jpeg)

There's a wide cycle path along the coast. It was probably because it was a weekday, but there weren't many people about. The open road, the good clear day, the mild wind off the sea. I felt light as anything, and deeply, quietly satisfied.

Soon the path turned away from the wide-open shore and ducked into a slightly narrower spot under an overpass. There was greenery all around, even on the overpass itself. It suddenly struck me as a good place to take a picture, so I stopped and took one.

![Greenery under the overpass](../../blog/%E6%B8%B8%E8%AE%B0/images/%E5%9B%AD%E5%8D%9A%E8%8B%917.8/92c6b67ecc89d3e825daecf18cb1d3d7.jpeg)

I kept riding and somehow found myself near the Garden Expo Park after all, so in I went for a look. I'd been taken there a few times in primary school, actually. When I thought back, I found I remembered nothing except a vague impression that there wasn't much to see. As soon as I went in, I couldn't help marveling at how lovely it was. What do kids know, haha.

![The lake at the Garden Expo Park](../../blog/%E6%B8%B8%E8%AE%B0/images/%E5%9B%AD%E5%8D%9A%E8%8B%917.8/322f1b04f8ae9a4d0718256f47864e2c.jpeg)

Right inside the gate was a broad lake reflecting the blue sky. Both were such beautiful colors. A bit farther in, I checked the map for the Jiangnan garden. I'd come across it on Xiaohongshu, and it was what I mainly wanted to see. The water towns of Jiangnan, their little bridges over running water, pavilions and towers, slender willows and lotuses, low houses in black and white. It's hard to imagine a gentler scene. I'd watched plenty of videos of Jiangnan online, and somehow grown to long for it quite keenly.

![The artificial lotus at the Jiangnan garden](../../blog/%E6%B8%B8%E8%AE%B0/images/%E5%9B%AD%E5%8D%9A%E8%8B%917.8/162732a10ad744093c5c5f919e3dcd34.jpg)

The park lived up to it. The Jiangnan garden was pretty much what I'd imagined. The sign seemed to say it had been donated by a local government somewhere in Jiangsu or Zhejiang. That gigantic artificial lotus, though, was a bit dreadful, I thought.

Actually, the first thing you see when you come in is the little courtyard on the left.

![The courtyard on the left](../../blog/%E6%B8%B8%E8%AE%B0/images/%E5%9B%AD%E5%8D%9A%E8%8B%917.8/965c052d82e87d9d073735b11e9493b3.jpeg)

The buildings have a charm of their own. Behind the door is the courtyard, with tall, slender bamboo and trees. Beyond it, you come to the crisscrossing waterways laid out just like a water town.

![A view of the Jiangnan waterways](../../blog/%E6%B8%B8%E8%AE%B0/images/%E5%9B%AD%E5%8D%9A%E8%8B%917.8/279e8f8eea904d2bcfb53c1aea953f67.jpeg)

![Another view of the Jiangnan garden](../../blog/%E6%B8%B8%E8%AE%B0/images/%E5%9B%AD%E5%8D%9A%E8%8B%917.8/3505679112ac2fe48f75508c7b34c128.jpeg)

![Water and buildings in the Jiangnan garden](../../blog/%E6%B8%B8%E8%AE%B0/images/%E5%9B%AD%E5%8D%9A%E8%8B%917.8/96bb023d4ad3ab8cb4cdcb7a8cf5068f.jpeg)

They even spray mist over the water for the effect.

![Mist on the waterway](../../blog/%E6%B8%B8%E8%AE%B0/images/%E5%9B%AD%E5%8D%9A%E8%8B%917.8/fa740d70db43444ef366573d6875837c.jpeg)

![Sunlight and mist over the lotus leaves](../../blog/%E6%B8%B8%E8%AE%B0/images/%E5%9B%AD%E5%8D%9A%E8%8B%917.8/5d55c2d86f53c3369462e74577d2ece3.jpeg)

In the sunlight, there seemed to be a bit of the Tyndall effect. Here and there you could see shafts of light hanging over the lotus leaves, as though in a land of immortals. A pity it wasn't time for the lotuses yet. It would have been even lovelier.

There were other quite lovely gardens. I saw a waterfall too, though I didn't take a picture. The Jiangnan garden had most of my heart by then. I wandered a little longer and left. I'll come back and look properly next time. It was getting late, after all.

When I got back, it felt as though the whole of me was a bit more alive. All of a sudden a Chopin sonata came to mind. It seemed to be egging me on to get the score out and have a go. But that's too hard, so never mind. It did make me want to work properly on the scherzo, though. Anyway, it stirred a good deal of life back into me and made me want to do a few things. That's good enough. Compared with the fog I'd been in before, it's quite a leap.
