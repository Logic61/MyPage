title: "A Fragment"
pubDate: 2023-10-25
category: Poetry
tags: [Poetry]
---

What fairy  
from what far place made this rouge  
and gave it to the clouds to wear?

Note: I came up with this one evening in my first year of high school when I saw the sunset. I thought it was fairly good and was rather pleased with it. I wanted to post it on Bilibili, but couldn't think what should come next, so I thought I'd put it up as a fragment. Then it turned out that Bilibili wanted two hundred characters. So I wrote two hundred characters of fluff just to post these three lines (laughs):

A fragment, to remember the evening clouds by.

Maybe in the not too distant future it'll grow into a poem

but right now I'm sleepy

and I have to write some more stuff because of the word count

this will do for now

at least I've written it down

so I won't forget it later

I could write this all in one line, actually

but doing it this way means I get to add these two lines

though I hadn't meant to

it somehow adds quite a lot of characters

“Hadn't meant to” and “somehow”

were written without meaning to, too

and somehow that adds a few more characters

is this what they call divergent thinking?

Like our English teacher

who'll be going over the text, and a turn of phrase

will set her off on a whole pile of similar things

and then we get through the lesson a bit more slowly

but we do learn a lot

likewise

I've written a lot of fluff

how odd

before I wrote “how odd”

I was at exactly 199 characters
