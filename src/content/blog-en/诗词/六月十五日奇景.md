---
title: "A Wondrous Sight on June Fifteenth"
pubDate: 2021-06-15
category: Poetry
tags: [Poetry]
---

The wind brushed the dancing willows, and my spirits rose;  
slowly, a veil of rose gauze spread over earth and sky.  

Bright clouds rose like butterflies into the blue;  
the pale sun hung like a boat bound for the immortals' home.  

Even the singing cicadas fell silent. Why strike up again?  
The restless crowd had quieted too. Who would break the hush?  

Who laid out a painting so far beyond compare,  
took the blue sky for a screen, and dyed it with cloud and mist?

Here too there was once a passage to make up the word count for Bilibili:

I don't see the point of a two-hundred-character minimum, but there's no getting around it. I can't make up the word count, so I'll just jot down a line or two here. I've moved this bit to the bottom so it gets in the way as little as possible.

I wrote this in 2021, when I was still in middle school. I think school had just let out for the afternoon. I'd been playing table tennis, and when I went upstairs and looked out the whole sky was ablaze with clouds. There was something romantic and lovely about it, I think, though I didn't really have anyone I could share the feeling with. So I wrote down the sight, for the day I might want to remember how things were. Looking at it today brings so much of that time back. I wonder whether, when I come across these offhand words someday, they too will make me remember. I imagine they will.
