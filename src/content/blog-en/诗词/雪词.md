---
title: "A Song of Snow"
pubDate: 2025-12-14
category: Poetry
tags: [Poetry]
---

Winter comes sighing, and how much sorrow with it.  
The willows on the bank are thinning;  
the old pines have gone white.

The winter crows circle and call, and will not settle.  
They must be wondering whether the slender boughs,  
with all that snow to bear, can bear them too.
