One modern poem, and one that’s, um, a little like regulated verse.

I

A modern poem for a Mid-Autumn assignment

Life is a lovely suite whose pieces flow one into the next.

Youth is one of its movements,

a green and reckless presto,

brief, glittering.

.

Even the loveliest music,

who remembers it by heart the first time they hear it?

So it is with the song of youth.

It sounds in a headlong, ardent rush

and leaves a jumble of broken phrases,

tangled together.

.

Youth is a work of some importance,

yet somehow we write it

just as we are learning to compose.

Still half in a haze,

we are already called upon

to bring difficult arts into play

and weave them into a splendid melody.

.

Youth is surely a strange polyphony:

tenderness and brilliance,

grief and delight,

opposed and intertwined,

intertwined and opposed.

Most people only ever hear one line.

But there is always an ear attuned to more

melodies,

each lending light to the others.

.

The key signature was set in the opening bars,

yet we can still,

with the urging of one note and then another,

open a new movement.

.

Let us not go on chasing the textures others left us

and write a tune whose ending you know from its first bar.

Youth should make a melody

that belongs to it alone,

with no other like it.

II

Written to put some recent feelings to rest

.

A wild goose crossing sky and earth, one life;  
the flowers and grasses cannot be as they were before.

Why lament the boundless sea of grief?  
Why ask how long the driving rain will fall?

Morning comes, dusk scatters, youth is gone;  
summer passes, winter comes, the soul departs.

Treasure, my friend, the time before your eyes.  
Sing one long song until the empty haze wears away.

Both written on September 11, 2022, between 19:30 and 20:16.
