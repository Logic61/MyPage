I wrote this while playing Genshin Impact, more or less because of the Raiden Shogun. I wrote it in English at the time. In middle school I translated the first line as “The seeds of story were brought by wind, and time would make they green.” Given how shaky my English was then, I suppose it wasn't too bad.

I entrust the seeds of stories to the wind.  
Time will make them sprout.

* * *

They skim past the sun in its unceasing rage,  
bathe in the gentle, clear light of the moon,  
rest among birds all in a clamorous flutter,  
come upon a beautiful, limpid pond.

* * *

Under the long, free wind,  
the sun lets go its anger, the birds fall still.  
The moon grows lovelier, the pond dearer to the eye.  
At ease,  
they rest in the shadow of the wind.

* * *

Until one day the wind dies.  
It is time for the flowers to open.  
The stories will come into bloom,  
with the longing of joy and sorrow twined.

* * *

Time moves on still, unfeeling.  
The flowers, frantic, seek a trace of the wind,  
if only for the space of a moment.

* * *

At last, one day, the wind comes again.  
Light as at the world's first opening  
kindles the hope of all things.  
Rejoice  
in the lovely flash of hibiscus at the treetops.  
Sing long  
of the rich scent of climbing fig in the water.

* * *

The spent flowers entrust the seeds of stories to the wind.  
Because of them, time will sprout.
