Learning mathematics sometimes feels like looking at flowers through mist. There is always a layer between me and the thing itself. But I came across Pikachu while browsing Bilibili, and when I listened to ZTF's problem sessions, I began to feel that the mathematics I've met so far, or rather the mathematics I ought to know, doesn't really ask for exceptional talent. Contrary to the usual picture, it takes time for it to settle, and experience before you get a feel for it. I always felt something was missing in the way I'd studied before, so I thought I'd try writing things down. Whether out of plain curiosity or practical need, I suppose I'd like to get better at mathematics. So I'm starting a series of little essays, a toolbox of sorts, or somewhere to keep my thoughts.
