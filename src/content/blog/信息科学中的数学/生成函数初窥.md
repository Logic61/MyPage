(1) 
$$
\begin{aligned}
E(e^{xt}) &= \sum_{k=0}^{n} e^{kt} \binom{n}{k} p^k (1-p)^{n-k} \\
&= (p e^t + 1 - p)^n
\end{aligned}
$$

(2) 由泰勒展开，
$$
e^{xt} = \sum_{i=0}^{\infty} \frac{t^i}{i!} x^i
$$
约定 $x^0 = 1$，取期望即有
$$
E(e^{xt}) = \sum_{i=0}^{\infty} \frac{t^i}{i!} E(x^i)
$$
已利用期望线性。

(3) 
$$
f(t) = E(e^{xt}) = \sum_{i=0}^{\infty} \frac{t^i}{i!} E(x^i)
$$
$$
f'(t) = \sum_{i=0}^{\infty} \frac{i \cdot t^{i-1}}{i!} E(x^i)
$$
$$
= E(x) + \sum_{i=2}^{\infty} \frac{t^{i-1}}{(i-1)!} E(x^i)
$$
$$
f'(0) = E(x) = n(p e^t + 1 - p)^{n-1} \cdot p e^t \Big|_{t=0}
$$
$$
= n p
$$

$$
f''(t) = E(x^2) = n(n-1)(p e^t + 1 - p)^{n-2} \cdot p^2 e^{2t}
$$
$$
\quad \quad \quad \quad \quad + p e^t \cdot n(p e^t + 1 - p)^{n-1} \Big|_{t=0}
$$
$$
= n(n-1) p^2 + n p
$$

(4) 
$$
E(e^{Yt}) = \sum_{k=0}^{\infty} e^{kt} \cdot \frac{\lambda^k e^{-\lambda}}{k!} = e^{-\lambda} \cdot e^{\lambda e^t}
$$
设 $g(t) = E(e^{Yt})$ 
$$
g'(0) = E(Y) = \lambda
$$
$$
g''(0) = E(Y^2) = \lambda + \lambda^2
$$
