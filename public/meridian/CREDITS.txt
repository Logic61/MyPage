Meridian Observatory

Original scene geometry, instrument models, engravings, book bindings and charts were authored for this project. The alpine panorama combines two generated ridgelines and an aerial valley plate in a full-sphere projection, with independent high-resolution charts for the summit and the valley floor. The vellum texture and high cirrus plate were also generated for the observatory. The valley mist uses a periodic procedural density field. The arrival image is a frame from the actual scene.
