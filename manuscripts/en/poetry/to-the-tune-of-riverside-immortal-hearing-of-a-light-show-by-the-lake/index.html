They tell me lights vie with one another
against the night beside the lake.
Alone, I cannot stir myself to brave the thickening cold.
On a thin screen I steal a glimpse of towers dressed in color.
Through all that shimmering light,
companions wander, side by side.

It is the turning of the year, a season full of gladness.
Trees of fire and silver flowers crowd the eye.
What am I to do with this shut window, and the quiet behind it?
Better to stand in the clear moonlight
and listen to the laughter drift by.
