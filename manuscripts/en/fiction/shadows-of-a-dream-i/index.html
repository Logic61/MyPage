Fragments of a dream.

“Then come over to my place tomorrow.” She pointed to the words on the paper.

I knew how much she meant by the invitation, how gravely she was asking one soul to lean on another. I looked at her face. There were little imperfections in it. She was beautiful all the same, her gentle eyes still fixed on the page. Was this the person I would spend the rest of my life with? For a moment I hung back. I don’t know why. Then, just as suddenly, I felt at ease.

“All right.” I nodded to her.

She’s three years older than me.

“Come on. It’s about to start.” She put down the white sheet of paper and looked over the edge. We were high up on the steps of a plaza ringed with tier upon tier of steps, like a stadium.
