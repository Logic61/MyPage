Out for a ride~

It’s a few minutes away by bike. Somehow in all these years I’d never been there once.

It’s probably no good to spend all your time shut up indoors. It makes you want to go out for a bit, too. In the afternoon I took a little yellow rental bike down to the sea to get some air.

The sun was blazing. The white clouds caught the light and glowed with a fuzzy, misted white, a pure and holy white, a fierce and lovely white.

The whole world had that bright palette you see in Miyazaki’s films, buoyant and brimming with life and the sense that things are just beginning.

There’s a little path down to the beach.

But when you get to the sign, it’ll tell you out loud that the shore is dangerous and you should turn back. I’d come all this way. How could I just go back? I followed the path all the way down. The wind came whooshing off the sea, the waves slapped the shore, and overhead was the blaze of the sun and great banks of white cloud. It really was a wonderful scene. As I got closer, five or six egrets went up all at once, spooking a whole patch of gulls and herons. I looked again and saw a gray thing still standing on the shore. A night heron, I think. I’d long heard of Master Night Heron and seen plenty of him on Bilibili, but this was the first time I’d seen one for myself. He was smaller than I’d imagined, though that shifty posture was exactly as I’d pictured it. I hadn’t got much closer before he spread his wings and flew off too. Pretty big detection radius, huh.

I didn’t get a picture of him. That one above is all I have. I caught the egrets by accident, very much out of focus.

The beach was strewn with stones of all sorts, so I thought of skipping them. I’d never tried. I picked one up and gave it a go. Plop. Straight to the bottom. I gave in and opened Bilibili to see how it’s done. I did finally get one to skip, though it didn’t go far. After a few tries, most got two skips or went straight under. Twice I got three. The stones in the videos are something else. They skitter across the surface like wheels and get twenty or thirty bounces in. There’s quite a bit to it, actually, but I wasn’t planning to stay long to work on my stone-skipping. I’d come on a rental bike and was outside the service area. I had to leave time to get back inside it. The school gave us a pass, but it only covers the first hour.

On the ride back I saw a few cows. So that’s why it smelled of manure on the way in.

I took a wrong turn on the way back and hit a dead end. Doubling back and finding my way took a while. Went over the hour. A tragic loss of one yuan.

Thinking back over the past two weeks at home for the holidays, I spent whole days feeling as if I had nothing to do. I didn’t want to study, didn’t even want to play games, and couldn’t be bothered to leave the house. Nothing interested me. I muddled along and two weeks of that blur went by just like that. It was awful, really. Every day I’d think of ways to kill time and want to do none of them. A novel would catch my interest now and then, and I’d read until I got sick of it and felt empty again. I’d scroll through social media for a bit, and soon that was boring too … Always after some instant satisfaction, a moment’s pleasure that couldn’t last and just sent me further into that endless emptiness.

Maybe I should get out more. Even after I came home I still didn’t particularly want to do anything and there was still that hollow feeling, but it had let up a good deal. There was a satisfaction that lasted. I felt a little more alive. And it felt so good on the road that without the time limit I might have ridden another ten or fifteen kilometers just for the fun of it. Tomorrow? Maybe. Though I should probably get the last driving theory test over with soon and be done with the whole license thing. Depends how I feel. There’s no great rush. I might as well do whatever makes me happy. It’s the holidays. I may as well slack off properly. I expect the winter and summer breaks from here on will all be pretty busy.
